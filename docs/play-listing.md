## English (default, en-US)

**App name** (21)
Chronobeat: Metronome

**Short description** (75)
Precise metronome: circular visuals, subdivisions, tap tempo, custom sounds

**Full description**
Chronobeat is a precise, good-looking metronome for practice, rehearsal and
songwriting.

Every click is scheduled on the audio clock, so the tempo stays steady at any
BPM, even with the screen dimmed or the app in the background. The visuals
follow what you actually hear, not when the sound was queued.

FEATURES
• Circular and linear visualisers that land exactly on each beat
• Tap tempo and a BPM dial with fine nudge buttons
• Time signature presets and a custom signature editor
• Subdivisions: eighths, triplets, sixteenths and more
• Accent, medium and normal beat levels, each with its own volume
• Bar counter and loop target, plus a practice timer
• Built-in sound presets, or load your own short audio files
• Visual sync offset for Bluetooth headphones
• Themes, node styles and knob finishes
• Haptic feedback on beats
• Works offline once installed
• English and Turkish

Keep the screen awake while it plays, and use it on the stand without ads or
accounts.
